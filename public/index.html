    Strain by week

    Trophies

    Last 52 weeks. The most points in a finished week wins it, and ties count for everyone tied.

    Seasons

    Each calendar quarter is a season, won by the most total points.

    Strain runs 0–21 and gets harder to raise the higher it goes. Points: commit 1, PR opened 4, PR merged 2, review 3, issue 1, private contribution 1. Contributions over 20 in a day score nothing, and each active day adds 5 points. Weeks run Monday to Sunday in UTC. History is recalculated from GitHub, so it reflects the current scoring rules and each person's current sharing setting. GitHub reports private work as one anonymous total covering commits, PRs, reviews and issues, and only for people who share it on their profile. It can't be split into the other columns.

Git Strain

    Breakdown

    Strain by week

    Strain runs 0–21 and gets harder to raise the higher it goes. Points: commit 1, PR opened 4, PR merged 2, review 3, issue 1, private contribution 1. Contributions over 20 in a day score nothing, and each active day adds 5 points. Weeks run Monday to Sunday UTC. GitHub reports private work as one anonymous total covering commits, PRs, reviews and issues, and only for people who share it on their profile. It can't be split into the other columns.
